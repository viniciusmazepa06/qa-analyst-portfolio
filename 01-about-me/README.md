# About Me

## QA Analyst | Software Quality Assurance

I work with a strong focus on software quality, manual testing, test analysis, and organized QA processes. I value understanding how a feature should behave, identifying scenarios that may affect users, and communicating findings in a clear and actionable way.

### How I approach quality

1. **Understand the requirement** — review expected behavior, business rules, and acceptance criteria.
2. **Identify risks** — consider critical user journeys, edge cases, integrations, and regression impact.
3. **Plan the tests** — define scenarios, data, preconditions, and expected results.
4. **Execute and investigate** — validate behavior, explore variations, and collect reproducible evidence.
5. **Communicate clearly** — document defects with steps, actual and expected results, and impact.
6. **Support confidence in delivery** — perform regression checks and share relevant quality observations.
