## About

I am a QA professional focused on software quality, structured testing, clear documentation, and reliable delivery. My approach combines attention to detail with a process-oriented mindset: understanding requirements, identifying risks, designing test scenarios, reporting defects with useful evidence, and supporting teams throughout the delivery lifecycle.

This portfolio is an evolving collection of independent, simulated projects created to demonstrate my testing approach. It does not contain confidential information, source code, customer data, or internal material from any employer.

## Core focus

- Manual and functional testing
- Test scenario and test case design
- Exploratory and regression testing
- Defect identification, reproduction, and reporting
- Requirements analysis and risk-based thinking
- Test documentation and evidence
- Quality processes and continuous improvement
- Collaboration with development and product teams
