**ID:** BUG-CART-001  
**Title:** Cart subtotal is not updated after changing product quantity  
**Severity:** High  
**Priority:** High

**Description:** In this simulated example, the cart quantity changes but the displayed subtotal remains unchanged.

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Add an available product to the cart.
2. Increase its quantity.
3. Review the subtotal.

**Expected result:** The subtotal reflects the current quantity multiplied by the unit price.

**Actual result:** The subtotal remains at the previous value.

**Evidence:** Not available — illustrative example, not an observed defect.
